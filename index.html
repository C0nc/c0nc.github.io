Yan Cui

Researcher @ ARC Institute, supervised by Jingtian Zhou

I work at the intersection of AI and biology, building computational methods to decode spatial tissue architecture from multi-omics and histopathology data. My research spans spatial omics foundation models, agentic AI systems for virtual cell modeling, and interpretable machine learning for single-cell genomics.
