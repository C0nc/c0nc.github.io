Yan Cui

PhD Candidate at the University of Pennsylvania

I am a PhD candidate at the School of Engineering and Applied Science (SEAS), University of Pennsylvania. My research focuses on bridging artificial intelligence with biological research, spanning from developing multi-agent systems for virtual cell modeling to creating interpretable machine learning methods for single-cell and spatial omics data analysis.
